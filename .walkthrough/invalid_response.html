Rehearsal Forecast — Inputs

  • Percentage Available For Rent must be between 0 and 1.

1. Capital

One-time capital outlays booked as Month 1 expenditures. The sum of these four fields is Total_Capital; that summary value and the derived Loan_Proceeds are shown on the results page.

2. Marketing

Four marketing line items. Each may be entered as a single constant applied to every month, or as an explicit 36-month schedule.

Search
Social
Other Marketing

Fourteen operational line items. Each may be entered as a constant or a 36-month schedule. Payroll_Tax is derived from Wages (Wages × 0.0765) and is shown on the results page — it is not a user input.

4. Building

Building geometry, the depreciable cost, and the occupancy schedule. Derived values Rentable_Sqft, Total_Rental_Units, Rented_Units, Rented_Sqft, and Monthly_Depreciation appear on the results page after Calculate.

Decimal in the inclusive range [0, 1] (e.g. 0.75 = 75%). Percentage Available For Rent must be between 0 and 1.
Depreciable building cost. Drives Monthly_Depreciation = Total_Building_Cost ÷ (Depreciation_Period_Years × 12).
Display-only. Captured for reporting; not used in any calculation in this phase.
Occupancy Rate Default schedule uses the built-in ramp Min(month × 0.10, 1.00); switch to Variable to enter 36 custom monthly rates.

Using the default 10%-per-month occupancy ramp: 10%, 20%, …, 100% for months 1 through 10 and 100% thereafter. Switch to Variable to enter 36 custom monthly rates.

5. Loan

Interest rate and term. Loan_Proceeds, Monthly_Loan_Interest, and Monthly_Loan_Principal are derived and appear on the results page after Calculate; they are not user inputs.

Decimal (e.g. 0.065 = 6.5% per year).

6. Taxes

Only Income_Tax_Rate is a user input. Payroll_Tax is derived at the fixed rate 0.0765 applied to Wages (Requirement 7.2) and appears on the results page.

Decimal in the inclusive range [0, 1] (e.g. 0.25 = 25%).

7. Owner Activity

Owner_Investment is a Month 1 cash contribution. Owner_Withdrawals is a single constant per-month value applied uniformly to every month (Requirement 1.6, Design Decision 8) — this section intentionally does not expose a Constant/Variable toggle.

Constant amount subtracted from cash every month, Month 1 through Month 36.

8. Forecast Controls

Opening cash balance and the month by which the business must reach a sustained cash-positive state through Month 36.

Integer in the inclusive range [1, 36].
